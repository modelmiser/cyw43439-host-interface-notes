# Framing and flow control

The SDPCM, BDC and command (CDC) headers as the drivers build and parse them, and the sequence, credit and flow-control rules each driver applies. Proptxstatus signalling, glomming and receive reordering are covered where a driver implements them.

## SDPCM header

- pico-sdk: SDPCM, BDC, CDC and credit handling are not found in these sources (its Bluetooth shared-bus files have no "sdpcm", "bdc" or "credit" name); everything below comes from embassy, c-driver, whd and linux. `pico-sdk/cybt_shared_bus_driver.c:1`
- Every driver starts an SDPCM frame with a 12-byte header: a 4-byte frame tag, then an 8-byte software header. linux: `SDPCM_HWHDR_LEN` 4, `SDPCM_SWHDR_LEN` 8, `SDPCM_HDRLEN` 12. whd: `SDPCM_HEADER_LEN` 12, `BUS_HEADER_LEN` 12. embassy and c-driver declare one 12-byte struct. `linux/brcmfmac/sdio.c:1330`, `linux/brcmfmac/sdio.c:1332`, `linux/brcmfmac/sdio.c:1333`, `whd/src/whd_sdpcm.c:51`, `whd/src/include/whd_sdpcm.h:54`, `whd/src/whd_sdpcm.c:99`, `embassy/structs.rs:75`, `c-driver/cyw43_ll.c:754`
- Sizes (compiled): whd's packed `sdpcm_header_t` is 12 bytes with an 8-byte software header; c-driver's unpacked `sdpcm_header_t` is 12 bytes with sequence at 4, channel_and_flags 5, next_length 6, header_length 7, wireless_flow_control 8, bus_data_credit 9, reserved 10. `whd/src/whd_sdpcm.c:85`, `whd/src/whd_sdpcm.c:99`, `c-driver/cyw43_ll.c:754`

### Host-side tx queues
