# Where the public drivers disagree

Digest of the points where the drivers disagree, grouped by subject; each comparison bullet gives every driver's side and a tag. Nothing here says which driver is right.

## Tags

- [layout] field positions differ.
- [size] same fields, different length sent or requested.
- [value] same field, different number.
- [order] same steps, different sequence or timing.
- [missing in X] one driver has no such step, field or definition.
- [naming only] same bytes, different name.
